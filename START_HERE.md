# Your new Ayurveda•Vita website: start here

This folder contains your new website: the same spirit as ayurvedavita.com, plus a booking calendar,
payment online (Stripe) or on site, and a private admin page to add your dates and see who booked.

You don't need to know anything about computers. **Claude Code will guide you through everything**, step
by step: trying the site on your computer, choosing its look, connecting your Stripe account, putting it
online and moving your ayurvedavita.com address to it.

Plan about 2 to 3 hours in total. You can stop at any time and continue another day.

## How to start

1. Unzip this folder somewhere easy to find, for example in your **Documents** folder.
2. Open a terminal **inside this folder**:
   - **Mac**: open Finder, right-click the folder > Services > **New Terminal at Folder**.
     (If you don't see it: open the Terminal app, type `cd ` with a space, drag the folder into the
     window, press Enter.)
   - **Windows**: open the folder in File Explorer, click in the address bar at the top, type
     `powershell` and press Enter.
3. Type `claude` and press Enter.
4. Write: **"Hi! Please help me set up my new website."**

Claude knows the whole plan and remembers where you stopped. To continue another day, do steps 2 and 3
again and write "Let's continue".

## Good to know

- When Claude asks for permission to run something on your computer, you can accept: it explains what
  each step does. If you are unsure, ask "what does this do?".
- **Never paste passwords or secret keys into the chat.** Claude will open the right file or tell you
  where to paste them.
- Have these ready: your **Stripe** login, your **WordPress.com** login (your domain is registered
  there), an email address, and a payment card for the hosting (about 5 USD per month).
- Screenshots of the four possible designs are in the `docs/designs` folder.
